Privacy Policy — Beaver Budget

Effective Date: November 18, 2026
Developer: Beaver Budget (com.beaverbudget.app)
Contact: See the developer support email listed on the Google Play Store page (or open an issue on the project repository).

1. Overview: Local-First & Private by Design

Beaver Budget is an offline-capable Canadian household budgeting and retirement planning calculator. By default, all financial inputs, budgets, accounts, properties, tax settings, and retirement projections remain 100% on your device.

  • We do not operate developer backend servers that receive, store, or process your financial data.
  • We do not create developer user accounts or track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting telemetry trackers.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

When you use Beaver Budget, the financial plan data you enter (such as income estimates, account balances, monthly outflows, mortgage/property figures, and tax/retirement preferences) is stored locally on your device using private on-device application storage.

You have full control over this local data:

  • You can inspect, edit, or delete any entry at any time inside the app.
  • You can export your entire plan as a local JSON backup file or restore/clear data from Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes locally stored app data managed within the application sandbox.

3. Optional User-Initiated Backups

Beaver Budget gives you two optional ways to back up or move your plan data—neither runs unless you initiate it:

  1. Local JSON Export / Import:
    Generates a plain JSON backup file on your device so you can save or share it using a destination you choose.
  2. Optional Google Drive Backup:
    If you explicitly choose to connect Google Drive backup in Settings → Backups, the app uses Google OAuth 2.0 (https://accounts.google.com/ and https://oauth2.googleapis.com/token) with limited Drive scopes (drive.appdata and drive.file) to:
    • Read your Google account email address / display name locally in the app so you can see which account is connected.
    • Store and restore your backup JSON directly between your device and your own personal Google Drive (inside your hidden app-data folder and, if enabled by you, a "Beaver Budget" Drive folder via googleapis.com/drive/v3).
    • OAuth tokens and Drive backup metadata are stored locally on your device. No intermediary developer server ever sees your OAuth tokens or your Drive backup contents.
    • You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from Settings → Backups, and revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions settings.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Beaver Budget does not sell, rent, trade, or share your financial or personal data with advertisers, data brokers, or third parties. Network communication only occurs when you manually initiate Google Drive backup/restore or open external browser sign-in for your own Google account.

5. Educational & Non-Advisory Disclaimer

Beaver Budget provides budgeting tools, cash-flow timing estimates, and Canadian tax/retirement projections (such as TFSA, RRSP, FHSA, CPP/QPP, OAS, and marginal tax bracket estimates) for personal educational and informational planning purposes only. It does not provide regulated financial, investment, tax, accounting, or legal advice, and does not provide banking, lending, money-transfer, or brokerage services. Consult a qualified financial planner, tax professional, or accountant before making financial decisions.

6. Children's Privacy

Beaver Budget is intended for a general adult audience managing household budgets and retirement plans and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If the app's data practices change in future updates, this Privacy Policy will be updated with a new effective date prior to release.
