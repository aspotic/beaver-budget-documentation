What Beaver Budget Covers

  • Active Budget & Cash-Flow Timing Track paychecks (federal/provincial tax, CPP/QPP, and EI ceilings), repeating bills, and monthly savings transfers. Spot potential shortfalls early and swipe bills as Paid when they clear.
  • Household Accounts & CRA Ownership Rules Organize Chequing, Credit, TFSA, RRSP, FHSA, and Taxable accounts for 1 or 2 adults plus 50/50 Joint accounts—enforcing CRA individual ownership on registered accounts while splitting eligible non-registered income accurately.
  • Properties, Mortgages & Optional Rentals Track primary residences, renewal countdowns, secured loans, and optional rental income/operating profiles without cluttering day-to-day budgeting.
  • Flippable Retirement Trajectory & Plan Checkup See savings grow year by year, compare Essentials vs. Nice-to-Haves vs. Luxuries milestones, model CPP/QPP and OAS timing, and run on-device plan checkups across solvency, longevity, and CRA room.
  • Private by Default No developer backend servers, analytics trackers, or ad SDKs. Export portable JSON backups anytime or optionally sync backups directly to your own Google Drive.
